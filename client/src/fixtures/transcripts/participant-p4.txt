"Yeah so my dad came home from hospital on Friday and, I mean, the actual care while he was there was really good. Everyone was lovely and they were really helpful with him.

It was more once we got home that I started getting a bit confused because we had, like, the paperwork they gave us when we left, and then there was information in the app as well, and I was trying to work out what we actually needed to do next.

Dad doesn't really use the app himself, so I was basically going through everything for him.

There was something on the paperwork about making an appointment with his GP, I think it said within a certain time, but then I looked in the app and the note there didn't seem to say exactly the same thing... or maybe I just wasn't reading it properly. I went back and checked it a few times.

And then the medication was probably the bit that worried me the most. There was a list there, but I couldn't really tell what had actually changed. Like, were these all the medicines he was meant to keep taking? Had something been stopped? Was one of them new? It wasn't really obvious to me.

I remember them talking about it before we left the hospital, but there was a lot going on at the time and, you know, you think you're gonna remember it all and then you get home and you're like... wait, what did they actually say?

So I rang the ward because I didn't wanna just guess. The person I spoke to was nice, but they said because Dad had already been discharged it would probably be better to check with his GP.

Then I called the GP clinic and they were like, um, we haven't actually received his discharge summary yet.

And that was the point where I was kind of thinking... okay, so who has the current information then? Because the hospital is telling me to ask the GP, but the GP hasn't got the information from the hospital yet.

I think I called the GP again the next day, and they still didn't have it at first. So for a little while we basically just had the paperwork, what was in the app, and whatever Dad and I could remember being told before he left.

That was probably the stressful part. Not because anyone was rude or anything, they were all trying to help, but the information just felt... scattered, I guess.

Eventually the GP got the discharge summary and they went through everything with us, and after that it made a lot more sense.

So yeah, it did get sorted. And like I said, Dad was actually really happy with the care he got in hospital. It was just the handover afterwards that felt a bit messy.

I think if there'd been one place that clearly said, okay, this is what's changed, this is what you need to do now, this is when you need to follow up, and this is who you call if something doesn't look right... that would've made it a lot easier."
