"So I went to my GP because I'd been having this issue for a while, and after we talked about it she said she wanted me to see a specialist.

She told me the referral would be sent through, so I just thought... okay, that's done, I guess I just wait for them to contact me.

I think it was about a week later and I still hadn't heard anything. At first I wasn't too worried because I know these things can take time, but then I started wondering if I was actually supposed to call someone myself.

So I rang the specialist clinic just to check, and they said they couldn't see any referral for me.

That kind of threw me because... I'd been told it was already sent. I didn't know if they just hadn't processed it yet, or if the GP hadn't sent it, or if I was meant to have a copy and send it myself or something.

I called the GP clinic after that and reception said they'd check what happened and get back to me. I waited a couple more days and didn't hear anything, so I ended up calling again.

Eventually they told me there'd been some problem with the referral and they were going to send it again.

But even then I wasn't really sure what happened next. Like, do I call the specialist again to make sure they've got it? Do I wait for them? How long am I supposed to wait before I know something's gone wrong again?

I think that was the most frustrating part. It wasn't even just the delay, it was more that I had no way of knowing where the referral actually was or who was responsible for following it up.

I just felt like I was going back and forth between the two places trying to work out what was happening.

Eventually the specialist clinic confirmed they had it, but by that point I'd already made quite a few calls myself.

It would've been much easier if I'd just gotten some kind of confirmation when the referral was sent, and maybe something telling me what to do if I hadn't heard anything after a certain amount of time."
