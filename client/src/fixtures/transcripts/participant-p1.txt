Participant P1

"I normally book appointments online because it's just easier than calling. Most of the time it's pretty straightforward. I can see what times are available and pick one that works for me.

The only thing I sometimes get stuck on is the appointment type. There can be a few different options and I'm not always sure what the difference is. Last time I just picked the standard one because it sounded closest to what I needed.

After I booked it, I got the confirmation straight away, so that part was fine. It had the date and time and everything. I think I would've liked a bit more information about whether I needed to do anything before the appointment, but it wasn't a huge issue.

The appointment itself was fine. I remember being asked for some information again that I thought I'd already put in online. I wasn't sure if that was normal or if the information just hadn't come through, but I just gave it to them again.

After the appointment, I got a notification saying there was an update in the portal. I went in and read it, but I wasn't really sure if I was supposed to do anything after that.

I think that was probably the most confusing part. The information was there, but I didn't know if I was meant to wait, book another appointment or contact the clinic. I checked the portal again later because I thought maybe I'd missed something.

In the end I called the clinic just to check. It wasn't a massive problem, but it would've been easier if the portal had just said something like what happens next or who to contact if you're not sure.

I'd still use the online booking though. It's much easier for me than calling every time, and I like having the appointment details there. There are just a couple of points where I feel like I need a bit more guidance."
